# Instructor / Facilitator Guide

The DIT handbook requires an instructor manual. It must be kept at the primary location and available during audits. This guide covers the provider's eLearning courses on the Escuela de Perforación platform. The language of instruction is Spanish.

## 1. Before a cohort starts

1. Check that the course appears in **Panel del instructor → Cursos y servidor** with the right hours, modules, exam size and IADC program number.
2. Enter the course and roster in the IADC accreditation database before learners start. Use the export **Registros por curso** as a source.
3. Share with each learner:
   - the platform link;
   - the support hours and contacts;
   - your own availability.
   Learners create their own accounts. Never create accounts for them or know their passwords.

## 2. During the course

- **Identity codes.** A learner may have no camera, for example when using Meta Quest headsets. In that case:
  1. Confirm their identity by video call or in person, with their ID document.
  2. Use **Aprendiz → Generar código de verificación**.
  3. Give them the 6-digit code. It works once and expires in 30 minutes.
- **Questions on content.** Answer learner questions during your published hours. Give technical answers based on the course content and the company procedures. If a learner reports an error in the content, log it as a complaint or comment so it enters the review cycle.
- **Monitoring.** Review **Aprendices** weekly:
  - active time against module minimums;
  - repeated knowledge-check failures;
  - identity photos compared with the enrollment photo;
  - pending complaints.

## 3. Final assessment

- The exam can only start after all modules are complete and a fresh identity verification.
- Do not provide answers or exam items to anyone. The question bank is confidential.
- When a learner uses all 3 attempts:
  1. Review their record.
  2. Contact them and recommend the modules to review.
  3. If appropriate, use **Habilitar examen**. The action is logged.

## 4. Certificates and records

1. On passing, the platform issues the provider record of completion immediately.
2. Upload the learner's completion to the IADC accreditation database. The database issues the official certificate or card.
3. Never upload a learner who has not completed all modules and passed.
4. If an identity mismatch or other irregularity is found:
   - stop;
   - inform the QA Manager;
   - do not issue or upload the certificate.

## 5. Course map (content summary)

The full syllabus with learning objectives and minimum times is in `03_course_syllabi.md`.

Each module follows the same sequence:

1. **Objectives.**
2. **Narrated lessons.** The instructor avatar narrates, with the key points on screen.
3. **3D practice**, where applicable. The learner does the practice in first person and passes with ≥ 70.
4. **Knowledge checks with remediation.**
5. **Minimum active time.** The learner can review the lessons or repeat the practice.
6. **Module complete.**

## 6. Equipment checks (provider location)

- **Before each session:**
  - computers and headsets charged;
  - browser updated;
  - the platform loads;
  - camera works for identity verification;
  - audio works.
- **Meta Quest headsets:**
  - lenses clean;
  - guardian boundary set;
  - controllers charged;
  - hygiene covers available.
- Record the check in the equipment log (`06_facilities_equipment.md`).
